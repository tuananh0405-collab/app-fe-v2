1. NOTIFICATIONS SCREEN
================================================================================

Description: The Notifications screen keeps you updated on important work-related events such as leave approvals, attendance reminders, and system announcements.

Instructions:

Figure 1: Notifications Screen

Step 1: View the Notifications Screen
    The system displays:
        App Bar:
            Title: "Notifications"
            Unread count: Shows "X unread" if you have unread notifications
            "Mark All as Read" button: Appears if you have unread items
            Back button: Returns to Home screen
        
        Notification List:
            Notification cards displayed from newest to oldest
            Each card shows:
                - Color-coded icon (green ✓, red ✗, orange 🕐, blue 📅, purple 📢)
                - Title (bold if unread, semibold if read)
                - Time ("Just now", "X minutes ago", "X hours ago", "X days ago", or "DD/MM/YYYY")
                - Priority badge ("HIGH" in orange or "URGENT" in red, if applicable)
                - Blue dot indicator on unread notifications
            
            Unread notifications: Thicker blue border with shadow effect
            Read notifications: Thin gray border without shadow

Step 2: View Notification Details
    Tap any notification card to view full details.
    
    A dialog appears showing:
        - Color-coded icon
        - Full title
        - Complete message content
        - Exact timestamp (DD/MM/YYYY HH:MM)
    
    Tap "Close" to return to the list.
    
    Note: Tapping a notification automatically marks it as read.

Step 3: Mark All Notifications as Read
    If you have unread notifications:
        Tap the "Mark All as Read" button in the top-right corner.
        A confirmation dialog appears asking: "Are you sure you want to mark all notifications as read?"
        Tap "Confirm" to proceed.
        
    Result:
        All notifications are marked as read.
        Blue dot indicators disappear.
        Unread count becomes 0.

Step 4: Refresh Notification List
    To check for new notifications:
        Pull down from the top of the list and release.
        A loading spinner appears at the top.
        New notifications (if any) will appear at the top of the list.

Step 5: Load Older Notifications
    The system automatically loads older notifications as you scroll.
    
    Simply scroll down through the list.
    When you reach near the bottom (200 pixels from end), older notifications load automatically.
    A small loading spinner appears while loading more items.

Step 6: Understand Screen States
    Loading State:
        Blue circular spinner appears while loading data.
        Wait for the notifications to load.
    
    Empty State:
        Bell icon with "No Notifications" message appears.
        Subtitle: "You're all caught up!"
        This means you have no notifications at this time.
    
    Error State:
        Error icon with "Error loading notifications" message appears.
        Tap the "Retry" button to attempt loading again.

2. NOTIFICATION TYPES
================================================================================

Description: Understanding different notification types and their visual indicators.

Instructions:

Leave Approval
    Icon: Green checkmark (✓)
    Meaning: Your leave request has been approved by your manager.
    Example: "Your leave request for 15/12/2025 has been approved"
    Action: Review the approved dates in the message.

Leave Rejection
    Icon: Red X mark (✗)
    Meaning: Your leave request has been rejected by your manager.
    Example: "Your leave request has been rejected"
    Action: Check the rejection reason and resubmit if needed.

Attendance Reminders
    Icon: Orange clock (🕐)
    Types: Attendance Reminder, Check-in Reminder, Check-out Reminder
    Meaning: Reminder to check in at the start of your shift or check out at the end.
    Examples: 
        "Please check in to start your work day"
        "Don't forget to check out before leaving"
    Action: Complete the check-in or check-out action immediately.

Leave Request
    Icon: Blue calendar (📅)
    Meaning: Confirmation that your leave request has been submitted.
    Example: "Your leave request has been submitted"
    Action: Wait for manager approval.

System Announcement
    Icon: Purple megaphone (📢)
    Meaning: Important system-wide or company announcements.
    Examples: 
        "System maintenance scheduled for tonight"
        "New policy update: Remote work guidelines"
    Action: Read carefully and take note of the information.

Other Notifications
    Icon: Gray info symbol (ℹ)
    Meaning: General notifications that don't fit other categories.
    Action: Review the notification content as needed.

TIPS & TROUBLESHOOTING
================================================================================

Tips for Best Experience:
    ✓ Check your notifications daily to stay updated on important events.
    ✓ Act promptly on urgent notifications marked with a red "URGENT" badge.
    ✓ Tap each notification to mark it as read and keep your list organized.
    ✓ Use the "Mark All as Read" button to quickly clear many unread notifications.
    ✓ Pull down to refresh before important tasks to ensure you have the latest updates.

Common Issues and Solutions:

Problem: Notifications won't load
    Solution 1: Pull down from the top to refresh the list.
    Solution 2: Check your internet connection (WiFi or mobile data).
    Solution 3: Tap the "Retry" button if an error message appears.
    Solution 4: Close and restart the app.

Problem: Unread count is not updating
    Solution 1: Pull down from the top to refresh the list.
    Solution 2: Close and reopen the Notifications screen.
    Solution 3: Wait a few moments and try again.

Problem: Not receiving new notifications
    Solution 1: Pull down to refresh and check for new items.
    Solution 2: Verify your notification settings in the app.
    Solution 3: Contact your manager or IT support if the issue persists.

Problem: Cannot load more notifications
    Solution 1: You may have reached the end of your notification history.
    Solution 2: Pull down to refresh the entire list.
    Solution 3: Check your internet connection.
