1. MY ATTENDANCE
================================================================================

Description: The My Attendance module allows employees to view their attendance history, work hours, and shift details. The screen provides a comprehensive overview of attendance records with filtering options by date range and status.

Instructions:

Figure 1: My Attendance screen

Step 1: View the My Attendance screen
    The screen is organized into sections:
        App bar with "My Attendance" title
        Filter section (date range, quick filters, status dropdown)
        Summary statistics card
        Shift history list
        Pull-to-refresh functionality

Step 2: View attendance summary statistics
    At the top of the content area, a summary card displays:
    
    Working Days (X/Y):
        X = Days you were present
        Y = Total working days in the period
    
    Work Hours:
        Total hours worked in the period
    
    Late / Early (X / Y):
        X = Number of times you arrived late
        Y = Number of times you left early
    
    Overtime:
        Total overtime hours in the period

Step 3: Select custom date range
    In the filter section, use the Start Date and End Date buttons:
        Tap "Start Date" to select the beginning of the range.
        Tap "End Date" to select the end of the range.
        The selected range must be valid (Start Date <= End Date).
        The data automatically updates upon selection.

Step 4: Use quick date range filters
    Tap one of the quick filter buttons for instant selection:
        This Week: Sets range to current week (Monday to Sunday).
        This Month: Sets range to current month.
        Last 30 Days: Sets range to the last 30 days.
    The summary statistics and shift list refresh immediately.

Step 5: Filter by status
    Tap the status dropdown to filter shifts by specific state:
        All: Shows all records.
        In Progress: Currently active shifts.
        Completed: Finished shifts.
        On Leave: Approved leave days.
        Absent: Missed shifts without check-in.

Step 6: View shift history list
    Below the summary, shifts are listed by date (newest first). Each card shows:
        Shift date and status badge
        Scheduled time (e.g., "08:00 - 17:00")
        Check-in/Check-out times (if recorded)
        Work hours, Overtime hours, Break hours
        Late/Early minutes (displayed in red if applicable)
        Shift notes (if any)

Step 7: Understand status colors
    Status badges indicate the attendance state:
        Orange (In Progress): Shift is currently active.
        Green (Completed): Shift finished with check-in and check-out.
        Purple (On Leave): Approved leave.
        Red (Absent): Scheduled but did not attend.

Step 8: Refresh attendance data
    To ensure you have the latest data:
        Pull down on the shift list area.
        A loading indicator appears.
        Release to trigger the refresh.
        The summary and list update with the latest information from the server.
