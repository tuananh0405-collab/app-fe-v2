1.1 Dashboard Overview
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Description: The Home Screen is the central hub of the application. It provides an immediate view of your daily work schedule, recent notifications, and quick access to essential modules.

Instructions:

Figure 1: Home Screen Layout

Step 1: Launch the Application
    After signing in, the Home Screen loads automatically.
    The top bar displays "Home" and a Notification bell icon.

Step 2: Check Welcome Header
    The top card displays:
        Greeting (Good Morning/Afternoon/Evening)
        Your Name
    Tap the Bell icon (top right) to open the full Notification Center.

Step 3: View Shift Schedule
    The "Shift Schedule" section shows your status for today:
    Case A - Current Shift:
        Displays your ongoing shift (e.g., "08:00 - 17:00").
        Shows "Current Shift" status.
    
    Case B - Incoming Shift:
        Displays your next scheduled shift for the day.
        Shows "Incoming Shift" label.
    
    Case C - No Shift:
        Displays "No incoming shift" if you are off duty or finished for the day.

Step 4: Check Latest Notifications
    A dashboard widget shows your 3 most recent alerts (e.g., Request Approved, Reminder).
    Swipe left/right to browse the summaries.
    Tap any notification to view the full message dialog.
    Tap "View All" to go to the Notifications screen.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1.2 Quick Actions & Navigation
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Description: Quickly navigate to frequently used features without searching through menus.

Instructions:

Step 1: Use Quick Action Buttons
    Located at the bottom of the dashboard.
    Tap to access:
        [Leaves]: Request time off or view leave balance/history.
        [Overtime]: Register overtime work or view request status.

Step 2: Refresh Dashboard
    To update the data (shifts, notifications):
    Pull down from the top of the screen and release.
    The screen will show a loading indicator and refresh the content.

Step 3: Bottom Navigation Bar
    Use the bottom bar to switch main modules:
    [Home]: Return to this dashboard.
    [Attendance]: Go to detailed attendance checking and history.
    [Work Schedule]: View your monthly roster.
    [Profile]: View account details and settings.
