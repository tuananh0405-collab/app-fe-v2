1. NOTIFICATIONS OVERVIEW
================================================================================

Description: The Notifications module keeps you updated on important work-related events such as leave approvals, attendance reminders, and system announcements.

Instructions:

Figure 1: Notifications Screen

Step 1: Access Notifications
    Tap the Bell icon on the Home Dashboard.
    OR tap "View All" from the dashboard widget.
    
Step 2: Understand the Interface
    The screen displays a list of your alerts.
    Top Bar:
        Title "Notifications" (Thông báo).
        Unread count (e.g., "3 chưa đọc").
        "Mark All as Read" button (Đọc tất cả).
    
    List Items:
        Each card shows the notification title, icon, and time received.
        Unread items have a bold title and a blue dot indicator.
        Urgent/High priority items have specific tags.

================================================================================
2. MANAGING NOTIFICATIONS
================================================================================

Description: View details, mark as read, and refresh your list.

Instructions:

Step 1: View Notification Details
    Tap any notification card in the list.
    A dialog appears showing the full message and timestamp.
    Tapping a notification automatically marks it as "Read".
    Tap "Close" to return to the list.

Step 2: Mark All as Read
    If you have multiple unread items:
    Tap "Read All" (Đọc tất cả) in the top right corner.
    Confirm the action in the popup dialog.
    All blue dot indicators will disappear.

Step 3: Refresh List
    To check for new notifications:
    Pull down from the top of the list and release.
    The list will reload with the latest data.

Step 4: Load More History
    Scroll down to the bottom of the list.
    If there are older notifications, the system will automatically load them.
