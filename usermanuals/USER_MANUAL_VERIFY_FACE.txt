1. FACE VERIFICATION OVERVIEW
================================================================================

Description: The Face Verification module is a critical security feature used 
primarily for Attendance Check-in. It ensures that the employee is physically 
present at the designated location by combining Facial Recognition with Liveness 
Detection, GPS Location verification, and Bluetooth Beacon validation.

Key Features:
- Real-time facial recognition
- Liveness detection (anti-spoofing) to prevent photo/video attacks
- Automatic integration with GPS and Beacon signals
- Visual guidance for face positioning
- Secure, hands-free automated verification

2. PERFORM FACE VERIFICATION
================================================================================

2.1 Access Verification Screen
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Description: The verification process is typically initiated when an employee 
attempts to check in for a shift. The system may enforce a time limit for 
verification completion.

Prerequisites:
- Active internet connection
- Bluetooth enabled (for Beacon detection)
- Location services (GPS) enabled
- Camera permissions granted

[Figure 01 - Verification Welcome Screen]

Step 1: System prompt
   - The system displays the Face Verification screen.
   - A countdown timer (e.g., "Verification window: 300s left") indicates 
     the remaining time to complete the process.

Step 2: Review instructions
   - Read any on-screen instructions.
   - Ensure you are in a well-lit area.
   - Ensure you are near the company beacon device.

Step 3: Start process
   - Tap the "Get Started" button to activate the camera.
   - If prompted, grant permissions for Camera, Location, and Bluetooth.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

2.2 Capture and Verify
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Description: This section covers the actual face scanning and automated 
authentication process.

[Figure 02 - Camera Face Scan]

Step 1: Position your face
   - Position your face within the oval frame on the screen.
   - Follow the color-coded visual guide:
     • WHITE: Ready / No face detected.
     • YELLOW: Face detected / Stabilizing (Hold still).
     • RED: Error (Face too far, spoof detected, or multiple faces).
     • GREEN: Face verified / Success.

Step 2: Liveness Challenge (Anti-Spoofing)
   - The system may ask you to perform a liveness check.
   - Follow the instruction: "Look at the camera and blink".
   - The oval may turn Blue/Primary color during this challenge.
   - Do NOT use photos or videos of a face; the system will detect this as a "Spoof".

Step 3: Stabilization
   - Once your face is detected and confirmed as real, the system tracks stability.
   - Message: "Hold still... X%"
   - Keep your phone steady and head still.
   - The oval will remain Yellow during stabilization.

Step 4: Automatic Capture
   - When stability reaches 100%, the oval turns GREEN ("Perfect!").
   - The system automatically captures your face image.
   - NO manual button press is required.

Step 5: Processing & Validation
   - The system automatically performs three checks in background:
     1. Beacon Validation: Verifies you are close to the office beacon.
     2. GPS Location: Records your precise location.
     3. Face Matching: Compares your captured face with your registered profile.
   
   - Status messages will appear:
     • "Validating beacon..."
     • "Generating face embedding..."
     • "Submitting face verification..."

Step 6: Completion
   - Upon successful verification:
     • Success message appears: "Face ID verified successfully!"
     • You will be redirected to the Success screen.
     • Your attendance check-in is recorded.

3. TROUBLESHOOTING
================================================================================

Problem: "Spoof detected! Please use a real face" (Red Oval)
Solution:
• Ensure you are not holding a photo or another phone in front of the camera.
• Ensure adequate lighting (avoid strong backlighting or very dark rooms).
• Remove masks or dark sunglasses.
• Look directly at the camera.

Problem: "Position your face properly in the oval" (Red Oval)
Solution:
• Move the phone closer or further away until your face fits the oval.
• Center your face in the frame.

Problem: "Verification window expired"
Solution:
• The time limit for checking in has passed.
• Tap "Back" or "Cancel" and restart the check-in process from the beginning.

Problem: "Beacon data not available"
Solution:
• Ensure Bluetooth is turned ON.
• Ensure you are physically close to the company's attendance device (Beacon).
• Wait a few seconds for the beacon to be detected before tapping "Get Started".

Problem: "Network Connection Issue"
Solution:
• Check your Wi-Fi or mobile data connection.
• Tap "Try Again" in the error dialog.

Problem: "Only one person should be visible"
Solution:
• Ensure no one else is standing behind you or in the frame.
• Use a plain background if possible.
