1. FACE VERIFICATION OVERVIEW
================================================================================

Description: The Face Verification module is used for secure Attendance Check-in. It validates your identity using facial recognition, liveness detection, GPS location, and Bluetooth beacons to ensure you are physically present at the office.

Instructions:

Figure 1: Face verification screen

Step 1: Access Verification
    The system displays the Face Verification screen with a countdown timer.
    Ensure you are in a well-lit area and near the company beacon.
    Tap "Get Started".
    Grant Camera, Location, and Bluetooth permissions if prompted.

Step 2: Position Face
    Position your face within the oval frame.
    Follow the color indicators:
        WHITE: Ready / No face detected.
        YELLOW: Face detected / Stabilizing (Hold still).
        RED: Error (Face too far, spoof detected, or multiple faces).
        GREEN: Face verified / Success.

Step 3: Liveness Check & Stabilization
    If prompted, perform the liveness challenge (e.g., "Look at the camera and blink").
    Once real face is confirmed, hold still while the system stabilizes.
    The oval remains YELLOW during stabilization (progress shown as %).

Step 4: Automatic Capture
    When stability reaches 100%, the oval turns GREEN.
    The system automatically captures your face image.
    NO manual button press is required.

Step 5: System Processing
    The system automatically validates based on:
        Beacon Signal (Must be near office device)
        GPS Location (Must be within allowed range)
        Face Matching (Must match registered profile)
    
    Case A - Successful Verification:
        Success message appears: "Face ID verified successfully!"
        User is redirected to the Success screen.
        Attendance check-in is recorded.
    
    Case B - Validation Failed:
        Error message appears explaining the issue (e.g., "Beacon not found", "Spoof detected").
        User is prompted to try again or check conditions.
